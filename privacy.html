Overview

This policy explains what information Plotbench ("the App") collects, how it's used, and your choices. Plotbench is built by MirodaLab ("we", "us").

Information We Collect

Plotbench is designed to run locally on your machine. The data you paste in and the plots you generate are processed and stored on your device — we don't receive copies of your data. If a future version adds optional cloud features, this section should be updated to describe what's collected and why.

How We Use Information

Because Plotbench runs locally, we don't collect or use the data you plot. Any information you send us directly (for example, by email for support) is used only to respond to you.

Data Storage & Security

Your data never has to leave your machine to use the App. If Plotbench later adds cloud sync or a hosted service, describe here where that data would be stored and how it's protected.

Third-Party Services

This website uses Giscus for comments, which stores comments as GitHub Discussions under GitHub's own privacy policy. The App itself does not currently integrate third-party analytics or advertising SDKs — update this section if that changes.

Children's Privacy

Plotbench is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
